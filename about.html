ABOUT TECHNEST

Technology should make everyday life easier.

TechNest is a fictional store created for this class project. It brings together useful devices for learning, working, staying active, and having fun.

Our mission

We want to make it easier to explore technology and compare products in one simple place. Whether you need a laptop for classes, a tracker for fitness, a tablet for creativity, or a VR headset for games, the right device should fit your needs.

What matters to us

  • Clear and useful product information.
  • Devices for different interests and budgets.
  • A simple shopping experience.
